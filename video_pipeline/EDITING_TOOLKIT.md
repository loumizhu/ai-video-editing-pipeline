# Documentary Editing & Custom Visuals Toolkit

## Purpose
The **Editing Toolkit** provides the creative, visual, and rhythmic standards for faceless documentary and tribute video essays produced within this pipeline. It translates script beats into compelling on-screen assets and native FilmCraft or DaVinci Resolve Studio timeline structures.

> **Absolute Constraint (Owner Mandate):** The owner strictly dislikes built-in NLE title, lower-third, and text presets. **Do NOT use built-in NLE title/lower-third/text presets anywhere.** Animated graphic families (lower-thirds, chapter cards, quote pull-outs, keyword pops) originate from our house presets (`presets/documentary-default/` or `presets/shorts-vertical/`) rendered via EffectCraft into ProRes 4444 `.mov` clips with alpha. Purposeful maps, article clippings with marker highlights, and static document cards are generated by our deterministic Python scripts in `tools/` and placed natively on tracks V3 (Graphics) and V4 (Overlays).

### 2.1 Transitions Library
- **Purpose:** Guide viewer attention between narrative beats without jarring cuts or unmotivated flashiness.
- **Trigger Rule:** Section breaks, chapter shifts, time jumps, and revelation peaks.
- **Anti-Overuse Rule & Density Cap:** Maximum 1 dynamic transition per 60s (≤10 per 10 min). Hard cut is the default for 85%+ of edits.
- **Narrative Beat to Transition Mapping Table (Names Matched to `Vcut_Effects_Library.md`):**

| Narrative Beat | Default Documentary Transition | Tribute Tone Variant (Video 010) | Duration | Paired SFX Category |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard Beat Cut** | Hard Cut (0 frames) | Hard Cut (0 frames) | 0.0s | None |
| **Time Jump / Scene Shift** | Soft Cross Dissolve (`tr.cross_dissolve`) | Soft Feathered Dissolve (`tr.cross_dissolve`) | 0.50s (12–15 frames) | None / ambient crossfade |
| **Chapter Change / Major Act** | Dip to Black (`tr.dip_black`) | Soft Dip to Black + 0.70s Fade (`tr.dip_black`) | 1.00s (24–30 frames) | Deep hit / sub boom |
| **Sudden Revelation / Shift** | Whip Pan (`tr.whip_pan`) or Push (`pipeline_ext.tr_push`)* | Soft Feathered Wipe (`pipeline_ext.tr_feathered_wipe`)* | 0.40s (10–12 frames) | Soft whoosh |
| **Archival Flashback / Memory**| Light Leak Sweep (`tr.light_leak`) | Warm Light Dissolve (`ovl.light_leak_sweep`) | 0.65s (16–20 frames) | Airy whoosh |
| **Archival Burnout Cut** | Film Burn Overlay (`tr.film_burn`) | Soft Grain Dissolve | 0.50s (12–15 frames) | Flame flare / shutter |
| **Audio-First Pacing Shift** | J-Cut / L-Cut (audio leads by 8–18 frames)| J-Cut (music/dialogue swells first) | 0.30–0.60s | Ambient room tone |
| **Technical / Corporate Crisis**| Glitch (`tr.glitch`, tech topics only) | Prohibited for Tributes | 0.20s (5–6 frames) | Static burst (subtle) |

*\*Note: `pipeline_ext.tr_push` and `pipeline_ext.tr_feathered_wipe` are pipeline extensions; in Vcut library native transitions are `tr.whip_pan` and `tr.ink_wipe`.*

- **`shot_plan` Fields (Canonical Vcut §3.5 Shape):**
  ```json
  "transitions": [
    { "type": "tr.cross_dissolve", "alignment": "center", "duration_frames": 12 }
  ]
  ```
- **How Applied in FilmCraft:**
  - Native FilmCraft transition: `filmcraft-cli --project <slug>.fcproj --save exec sequence.applyVideoTransition '{"clip": <id>, "effect": "cross_dissolve", "frames": 12, "edge": "in"}'` (verified; dynamic change concentrated in last 1–2 frames).
  - Overlay clip on V4: Pre-rendered ProRes 4444 / PNG alpha transition clip.
  - Callout in/out fades: Animated ProRes 4444 `.mov` with baked alpha (standard) or native opacity keyframing (`effects.setParam` + `effects.setInterpolation`; Revision 2026-11-01).
- **Fallback (Bypass):** Fall back to standard 12-frame Cross Dissolve; if CLI fails, hard cut with audio J-cut.
- **Worked Example (ILLUSTRATIVE, not verified):** End of Act 1 into Act 2: 1.0s `tr.dip_black` on V1 paired with deep hit on A3, music ducked.
- **Acceptance Check:** Zero unmotivated transitions; total dynamic transitions across 10 min ≤ 10.

### 2.3 Purposeful Maps (Only When Geography IS the Point)
- **Purpose:** Visually communicate spatial relationships, journeys, scale surprises, and territorial facts.
- **The Core Rule:** A place merely mentioned does **NOT** get a map. A map is triggered **ONLY** when geography is the core story beat:
  1. A documented journey or route between physical locations.
  2. A distance or scale surprise that changes the viewer's understanding.
  3. A border, jurisdiction, or proximity fact central to the narrative.
  4. A before/after territorial change.
  5. A multi-pin cluster of simultaneous events.
  6. A connection between two places that links disparate narrative threads.
- **Anti-Overuse Rule & Density Cap:** Maximum 2 maps per 10 minutes (maximum 4 in a 25-minute documentary).
- **Map Types (All 6 Formally Implemented & Distinct):**
  - `route_draw` (`map.route_draw`): Animated gold route drawing along route legs (straight lines between stops) between verified waypoints with computed haversine direct and route-sum distance badges.
  - `locator_zoom` (`map.location_pin`): Wide regional context map with glowing target reticle matching exact ground extent and a dedicated zoomed high-resolution local detail inset panel (620x440) with independent scale bar.
  - `distance_line`: Direct measurement vector line between two points with dimension tick marks and exact straight-line distance badge.
  - `multi_pin_cluster`: Multiple coordinate pins highlighting related event sites with numbered badges (1..N), repulsion-based label collision avoidance, and dedicated site index legend panel (zero route lines).
  - `comparison_inset`: Primary region canvas plus a secondary territory comparison inset panel at identical 1:1 ground scale ($k$ px/rad) with matching scale bar.
  - `hidden_connection`: Stylised curved connection arc (screen-space curve, not geodesic) with pulsing dashed styling, Origin [A] and Destination [B] badges, and neutral direct distance badge.
- **Cartographic Integrity Note (AGENTS §2):** Connection and route labels may only state geography that is verified (distance, endpoints), never an unverified relationship or assertion of secrecy. Labels must use neutral terminology (`CONNECTION`, `DIRECT DISTANCE`).
- **Cartographic Data & Licensing:**
  - Sourced strictly from Natural Earth 1:50m Vector Data (Public Domain), stored locally in `tools/data/` with `LICENSE.txt`.
  - Explicit ban on scraped Google Maps imagery or fake procedural geography.
  - Mandatory cartographic attribution line on-screen ("Cartography: Natural Earth 1:50m Vector Data (Public Domain)") and logged in `03_media/asset_registry.json`.
- **`shot_plan` Fields:**
  ```json
  "map": {
    "type": "route_draw",
    "title": "THE HUDDERSFIELD JOURNEY",
    "subtitle": "Geographic Scale & Early Theatrical Circuit",
    "points": "53.67,-1.69,Mirfield (Birthplace);53.64,-1.78,Huddersfield (University);51.50,-0.12,London (West End)",
    "alpha_overlay": true,
    "duration_s": 5.0
  }
  ```
- **Generation:** *(Working directory: Videos-Scripts/<NN>_<Slug>/)*
  ```bash
  python ../Video_Pipeline/tools/render_map.py \
    --title "<Title>" --subtitle "<Sub>" --points "<pts>" --out "07_Video_Edit/graphics/<slot>_map.png" [--alpha-overlay] [--frames N]
  ```
- **FilmCraft Placement:** Placed on track V1 (if full-frame) or track V3/V4 (if transparent alpha overlay) via `timeline.place`.
- **Fallback (Bypass):** Static vector map card; if script fails, clearly labelled 'SCHEMATIC, NOT TO SCALE' placeholder that never prints an uncalculated distance.
- **Worked Example (ILLUSTRATIVE, verified on map_sample.png):** Multi-leg route from Mirfield via Huddersfield to London West End with computed haversine distances: Direct A->Z straight-line `163.8 miles (263.6 km)` and Route length (sum of legs) `167.7 miles (269.9 km)`. - **Acceptance Check:** Geographic coordinates verified; projection aspect undistorted (identical scale k in px/rad on both axes); haversine distance computed with distinct direct and route-sum labels; projection scale bar included; Natural Earth attribution present.

### 2.6 Organic Celluloid Film Grain Synthesis (Moving Beyond Screen Overlays)
- **Purpose:** Apply an authentic, organic, texturized film grain that mimics physical chemical celluloid (silver halide crystals suspended in gelatin) and completely eliminate the artificial "digital static" or milky contrast caused by legacy screen-mode overlays.
- **Why Legacy "Screen Mode" Particle Overlays Fail:**
  - *Mathematical Flaw of Screen Mode:* The Screen blend formula is $1 - (1 - \text{Base}) \times (1 - \text{Blend})$. It is strictly additive. In deep shadows and black bars, Screen mode lifts zero values to gray, resulting in milky, washed-out shadows and destroyed contrast.
  - *Particles vs. Celluloid Emulsion:* Floating particles, dust specks, and static pixel noise look like dirty optics or digital sensor static. True film grain is optical density variation embedded inside the photographic emulsion.
  - *Modern Rule:* Grain must be applied via native NLE effect engines (FilmCraft native noise / DaVinci Resolve Studio Film Grain OpenFX), procedural FFmpeg luma-masked synthesis, or continuous celluloid plates on Track V4 blended exclusively in **`Overlay`** or **`Soft Light`** (never `Screen`).

#### 🎛️ Core Mathematical Rules for "Non-Digital" Grain
1. **Luminance Masking (The Golden Rule):** Real film grain lives heavily in the midtones (~18% to 70% luma). It is physically suppressed in pure whites (>85% highlights) due to emulsion saturation and falls off sharply in pure blacks (<15% shadows) where minimal halide crystals developed.
   - Parabolic midtone weighting formula: $W(Y) = \max\left(0, 4 \times Y \times (1 - Y)\right)^{1.2}$ across normalized luma $Y \in [0, 1]$.
   - Uniform grain across all luminance levels is strictly prohibited.
2. **Monochromatic Base (Zero Color Noise):** Chrominance saturation / color noise must be set to **0%**. Real film grain is structural silver halide, never RGB rainbow pixel noise.
3. **Temporal Randomness (Exact Frame Rate Sync):** Noise patterns must animate continuously at the exact sequence frame rate (23.976, 24, or 30 fps CFR). Frozen noise or short visible loop cycles are strictly prohibited.

#### 🎬 Narrative-Driven Grain Profiles
Select the grain profile that matches the narrative era and emotional intensity (defined in [`presets/color/film_grain_profiles.json`](presets/color/film_grain_profiles.json)):
- **Profile A: Clean Modern Cinema (The "Fincher" Look - `grain.clean_35mm`):**
  - *Context:* High-end corporate, sleek drama, modern thriller, sci-fi. Gives organic depth to pristine digital sensors without looking retro.
  - *Stock:* Fine-grain 35mm (Kodak Vision3 50D).
  - *Settings:* Microscopic grain size (`0.05 - 0.10`), ultra-subtle intensity (`2% - 4%`), confined strictly to tight midtones.
- **Profile B: Classic Hollywood / Cinematic Drama (`grain.classic_35mm`):**
  - *Context:* Character-driven stories, indie films, period dramas, human interest. Warm, nostalgic, deeply human.
  - *Stock:* Standard 35mm (Kodak Portra 400 / Fujifilm Eterna 500).
  - *Settings:* Balanced grain size (`0.15 - 0.25`), moderate intensity (`6% - 10%`), softened edges (`0.10 softness`) so crystals feel optical.
- **Profile C: Gritty / Documentarian / Action (The "16mm" Look - `grain.gritty_16mm`):**
  - *Context:* Raw sports documentaries, war films, urban street style, intense investigative turns. Immediate, tactile, authentic.
  - *Stock:* 16mm celluloid (Kodak Vision3 500T).
  - *Settings:* Medium-large grain size (`0.35 - 0.45`), heavy intensity (`12% - 18%`), higher contrast texture.
- **Profile D: Vintage Archive / Memory Fragment (The "8mm" Look - `grain.vintage_8mm`):**
  - *Context:* Deep historical memories, home-video aesthetic, dream sequences, stylized archival recreation.
  - *Stock:* Super 8 / 8mm film.
  - *Settings:* Large chunky grain (`0.60 - 0.80`), aggressive intensity (`20% - 30%`), plus subtle spatial gate weave ($\pm 1\text{px}$ camera jitter).

#### 💡 Advanced Execution Pro-Tips
- **Upscale Check (4K Resolution):** Grain size is resolution-dependent. For 4K (3840x2160) canvas, scale grain size by **~1.85x**; otherwise fine 1080p grain dissolves into digital compression blur.
- **Sharpening Pipeline Order:** Always follow the strict chain: `[Grade / Look LUT] -> [Film Grain Synthesis] -> [Master Sharpening]`. Never sharpen footage after adding grain, as edge sharpening algorithms detect grain boundaries and create harsh digital static.
- **Compression Prep (YouTube Buffer):** Web codecs (VP9/AV1) compress away fine high-frequency texture. Slightly over-compensate master export intensity by **+2%** to survive delivery encoding.

#### 🛠️ NLE & Toolchain Execution
1. **FilmCraft Native Effect:**

   { "id": "effects.setParam", "params": { "clip": "<id>", "effect": "noise", "param": "monochrome", "value": true } }

## 4. Edit Rhythm & Pacing Standards

### 4.1 Visual Change Cadence
- **Standard Visual Cuts (`documentary_standard`):** Every **3.5 to 4.5 seconds** (84–108 frames @ 23.976fps, 105–135 frames @ 30fps). Other presets (`slow_reflective`, `tense_fast`, `shorts_vertical`) change the numbers; see [PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md](PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md) §5.
- **Still-Hold Budget (Revision 2026-11-09, replaces "never hold an unmoving frame >4.5s", which nothing enforced):** a *still run* (one picture, or several pictures of the same idea, ended only by a distinct shot) has a **soft cap of 4.5 s** and a **hard cap of 6.0 s** on `documentary_standard` (slow 6.5/8.0, tense 3.5/4.5, shorts 3.0/4.0; tighter on intensity 4-5 beats; reading plates +1 s). Between soft and hard cap the still must carry enrichment events so no event-free stretch exceeds 3.5 s. Past the hard cap: add a distinct shot (cutaway, plate), never a repeat. A 12 s still is never valid. Enforced by `still_hold_qa.py`, `beats_qa.py`, `asset_audit.py`. Full rules, enrichment ladder and fix order: [PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md](PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md) §2-3.
- **Text & Quote Cards Exception (Video 010 Calibrated):** **5.0 to 6.0 seconds** total duration.
  - Line 1 (primary text) held alone for ≥2.5s for dedicated reading comprehension.
  - Line 2 (reveal text) animates in underneath for 2.5–3.0s with soft feathered wipe / fade.
- **Article & Document Displays:** **4.5 to 5.5 seconds** allowing viewer to read the headline before marker sweep.
- **Character Callouts:** **4.0 to 5.0 seconds** hold time, fading out 0.5s before shot cut.

### 4.2 Variety Budget (Per Minute)
To keep documentaries engaging and prevent "footage fatigue", every 60 seconds must adhere to the variety budget:
- At least 1 archival B-roll shot.
- At least 1 graphic asset (callout, map, article, date strip, or stat card).
- No more than 3 consecutive shots of the same media type.
- Music bed dynamically responsive to emotional beats and voiceover ducking.
- **No still run longer than its Still-Hold Budget** (§4.1); every still run past the soft cap carries at least one enrichment event per ~3.5 s (annotation, detail crop, keyword, stat, stamp...). Choose the method from what the line says (narration → method table, [PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md](PACING_AND_ENRICHMENT.md) §3.2): cut first, enrich second, decorate never.
